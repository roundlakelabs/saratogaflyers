# How to update the Saratoga Flyers website

You can change the website's text, photos, costs and events from your web browser. You
don't need to install anything, and you can't break the site. Every change is saved and
can be undone.

## 1. Accept your invitation (first time only)

1. The site maintainer will invite you by email. The email comes from **Pages CMS**
   (the Round Lake Labs editor).
2. Click the link in the email. It takes you to **cms.roundlakelabs.com**.
3. Sign in with the **same email address** the invitation was sent to. Pages CMS will email
   you a sign-in link. Click it. You don't need a password or a GitHub account.

## 2. Signing in later

1. Go to **https://cms.roundlakelabs.com**.
2. Sign in with your email address, then click the link Pages CMS emails you.
3. Click the **saratogaflyers** site.

## 3. What you can edit

The menu on the left lists everything you can change:

| Menu item | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Events** | The "Upcoming Events" box on the home page |
| **Home page** | Home page text and photo gallery |
| **Our Plane** | Specs, equipment list and photos on the Our Plane page, plus the summary on the home page |
| **Membership & costs** | Dues, capital investment, hourly rate, and the membership text |
| **Contact page** | The Contact Us page text and photo |
| **Resources page** | The quotes and link lists on the Resources page |
| **Site settings** | Club name, contact email, footer text, member calendar link |

## 4. Add an event

1. Click **Events**, then **Add an entry** (or the **+** button).
2. Fill in:
   - **Event name**, e.g. "Monthly member meeting"
   - **Date and start time**
   - **Location** (optional)
   - **Link for more details** (optional). This must be a full address starting with `https://`.
   - **Description**. Type it like you would in an email. The toolbar has bold, links and lists.
3. Click **Save**.

The "Upcoming Events" box only appears on the home page when at least one event is coming
up. The day after an event, it disappears from the website by itself. You don't need to
delete it.

To change or cancel an event, click it in the **Events** list, then edit it and **Save**, or
delete it.

## 5. Change costs, text or the plane's details

1. Click the item in the menu, e.g. **Membership & costs**.
2. Change what you need. Costs appear exactly as you type them, e.g. `$75` or `$50/hour dry`.
3. Click **Save**.

Lists (equipment, specs, links, photos) have an **Add** button at the bottom. You can
**drag** items to reorder them, and use each item's menu to remove it.

## 6. Add or change a photo

1. Open **Home page** (gallery), **Our Plane** (plane photos) or **Contact page**.
2. To add a photo to a gallery, click **Add** at the bottom of the photo list. To change an
   existing photo, open it and click its current picture.
3. Pick a photo already on the site, or **upload** a new one from your computer.
   - Landscape photos look best. Photos are cropped to a 4:3 shape.
   - Please keep photos under about 2 MB. Most phone photos are fine, but very large ones
     slow down the site.
4. Fill in the **Caption** and the **Photo description**. The description is read aloud
   to visitors who can't see the image, e.g. "Club members washing N73195 on the ramp".
5. Click **Save**.

You can also manage all uploaded files under **Media** in the left menu. That's also where
you upload a PDF if you want to link to it from the Resources page.

## 7. How long until I see my change?

About **1–2 minutes** after you click Save. Then reload the website. If it still looks
old, wait another minute and try a "hard refresh": **Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows, or
**Cmd+Shift+R** on a Mac.

## 8. If something looks wrong

- **Don't worry.** Every save is kept in the site's history, and the maintainer can put
  back any earlier version.
- If you made a mistake you can see, just edit it again and save.
- If the site doesn't update after 10 minutes, or something looks broken, contact the
  site maintainer. Tell them what you changed and roughly when.
