We look forward to hearing from you!

Saratoga Flyers, Inc. is a 501(c)(7) tax-exempt not-for-profit corporation organized to allow its members to engage in non-commercial flying for fun and to gather together informally for social activities and discussions pertaining to our flying experiences and techniques so as to benefit all members from an educational as well as recreational standpoint.

The Club owns a 1976 Cessna Skyhawk (C172) which is hangared at Saratoga County airport (5B2). The board & members meet monthly to discuss the operation of the Club, as well as to do a little hangar flying with one another. The time and location of meetings vary so as to offer opportunities for members with diverse personal schedules to attend.

If you are interested in learning more about Saratoga Flyers, or have any questions, please use the button below to contact us.
