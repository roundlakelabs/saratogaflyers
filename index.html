Membership Opportunity

Membership is open to pilots interested in non-commercial flying for fun and sharing their flying experiences. There is no minimum flight experience required for certificated pilots, and active student pilots are now eligible to be members.

The club does not provide flight instruction but may accept student pilots as members. It is the responsibility of the student pilot member to work with a Certified Flight Instructor who has been accepted by the club for purposes of individual flight training.

If you are interested in joining us, or want more information, please let us know through the Contact Us page.
